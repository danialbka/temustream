# Code of conduct

TemuStremio should be a place where people can work together without being
harassed, belittled, or pushed out.

Be respectful and specific. Critique the code or decision, not the person.
Assume honest mistakes, make room for different levels of experience, and stop
an interaction when someone asks for space. Harassment, threats, hate speech,
sexualized attention, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in
project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, reject contributions,
or limit participation when needed to protect the community. Decisions should
consider context, impact, and whether harmful behavior is repeated.

To report conduct privately, use the repository's private vulnerability
reporting route and state that the report concerns community conduct. If that
route is unavailable, open a short public issue asking a maintainer for a
private contact method without naming people or describing the incident.
